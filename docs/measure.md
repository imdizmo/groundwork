# Measure

**Measure** (`M`) measures between two points: click one, then the other. The panel gives the distance along the
ground and straight, the height difference, the slope, each point's height and depth under the water, and how long
the way takes walking (1.4 m/s on the flat) and running (5 m/s, a game character's): slower uphill and on steep
downhill, fastest on a gentle way down, swum at a metre a second where it is under water. Set the second point on a
town and the first on a camp to see how far apart they feel; compare it with [played sessions](playtest.md).

`Esc` clears the measure. The tape stays on the map until another measure or another map.
