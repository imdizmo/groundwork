# Measure

**Measure** (`M`) measures between two points: click one, then the other. The panel gives the distance along the
ground and straight, the height difference, the slope, each point's height and depth under the water, and how long
the way takes walking (1.4 m/s on the flat), running (5 m/s, a game character's) and riding (8 m/s, a horse's
canter): slower uphill and on steep downhill, fastest on a gentle way down, swum at a metre a second where it is
under water. The ground counts too, by its layer's name: marsh, swamp, bog or mud at half the pace, snow or ice 0.6,
sand 0.75, rock, scree or stone 0.8, forest or wood 0.85, a road, path or track and the map's kept
[roads](paths.md#kept-roads) 1.1; any other layer as grass. Set the second point on a
town and the first on a camp to see how far apart they feel; compare it with [played sessions](playtest.md).

`Esc` clears the measure. The tape stays on the map until another measure or another map.
